## Transcript
In this town, everyone receives a mysterious package at midnight. Nobody knows who delivers them. And there's only one
rule. Never open a package that has your own name written inside. One night, a girl named Emily heard three knocks at
her front door. Outside was a small black box. There was no return address. She carefully opened it. Inside was a tiny
model of her house. Every room looked exactly the same. Even the furniture. But then Emily noticed something strange.
Inside the miniature bedroom was a tiny version of herself. And it was moving. Emily watched as the tiny figure walked
toward the bedroom window. Then it looked directly up at her. Suddenly, the miniature house began shaking. A gigantic
hand appeared outside its window. Emily screamed. Because the hand looked exactly like hers. Then she heard three
knocks. Not at her front door. But from somewhere above her house. And the entire sky began opening like a cardboard box.

## Shots that depended on a drawn expression
A real avatar keeps its Roblox face: `setFace()` and blinks don't show on it. Two beats were written for an expression:
* **Emily's scream (47.6):** her mouth doesn't open. Played with a jump back, both arms thrown up, a push-in and camera shake, over the sound.
* **The tiny figure looking up (39.9):** no change of expression. Played with framing: she turns and tips her head until she looks into the lens, then the reverse shot. The fixed face makes this one creepier.

Also: `lookUp` tips the whole head, so past about 0.5 the face turns to a profile; keep it low in front views.

## Beyond the script
The script ends on "the entire sky began opening like a cardboard box". The picture adds Emily's giant face looking in
over the last few seconds, after the narration, to pay off the hand. It has no words added.
